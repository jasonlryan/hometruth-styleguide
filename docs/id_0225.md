Below is an updated version of your pre-seed deck with added emphasis on the size of the opportunity based on high-intent digital traffic. This version weaves in the research findings and highlights the massive potential in the UK market.

1.  Problem

The Real Estate Decision-Making Gap

Outdated Models: Traditional models rely on demographics, static credit scores, and outdated filters. They miss the behavioral and emotional drivers influencing home purchases.
Widespread Regret: 68% of buyers regret their purchase decisions due to misaligned expectations.
Inefficient Agent Time: 42% of real estate agents’ time is wasted on unqualified or misaligned leads.
Current Solutions Fall Short:

PropTech Limitations: Existing platforms focus on listings and price comparisons, not personalized, data-driven decision support.
Mortgage Models & Risk Assessments: They fail to factor in emotional triggers, leaving buyers exposed to costly mistakes.
Vendor Mismatch: Service providers lack predictive insights to reach homeowners at the right moment.

2.  Solution

AI-Powered Personal Property Assistant

HomeTruth uses behavioral AI and psychographic profiling to:

Empower Buyers: Help first-time and experienced buyers understand the full impact of their property decisions before committing.
Guide Homeowners & Landlords: Offer long-term property management insights, maintenance alerts, and refinance opportunities.
Match with Vendors & Financial Products: Connect users with pre-vetted service providers and tailored financial offers.
Key Features:

Real-Time Attitudinal Profiling: Analyzes user behavior, anxiety, risk tolerance, and decision fatigue.
Personalized AI Insights: Delivers proactive property management alerts and actionable financial opportunities.
Vendor & Financial Marketplace: Uses behavioral data to match homeowners with the right contractors, mortgage deals, and insurance products.

3.  Why Now?

AI in PropTech is Booming: While most solutions remain transactional, the market is ripe for an advisory, AI-driven approach.
Complex Homeownership Environment: Inflation, climate risks, and regulatory changes demand personalized, real-time financial insights.
Digital High-Intent Traffic: UK property platforms (e.g., Rightmove, Zoopla) receive hundreds of millions of visits monthly—evidence that homebuyers (especially first-timers) are actively researching and making decisions online.

4.  Market Opportunity

High-Intent Digital Traffic & Market Size:

First-Time Homebuyers:
Major UK portals like Rightmove and Zoopla record 140–200 million visits per month.
Young, first-time buyers (typically aged 25–34) make up a significant portion of this traffic.
Even a small capture rate can translate into millions of engaged potential users.
Broader Homebuyer Interest:
Overall UK property search traffic translates into roughly 3+ visits per capita per month, indicating deep market engagement.
Landlords & Second-Home Owners:
Approximately 2.8 million private landlords in the UK and nearly 772,000 second-home owners contribute significant digital activity on rental and property management platforms.
This group not only searches for properties but actively uses online tools for managing and leasing their assets.
Market Sizing:

TAM (Total Addressable Market): Over £500B in global real estate transactions and home services.
SAM (Serviceable Available Market): Around £30B in personalized home management and AI-driven advisory services.
SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market): A potential £5B+ opportunity in subscription-based PropTech solutions and vendor lead generation—underscored by the vast, high-intent online traffic in the UK.

How It Works:

Aggregates user responses, search behaviors, and in-app interactions to create a detailed psychographic profile.
Ongoing Guidance:
Provides tailored advice—from property purchase decisions to refinancing, maintenance, and sale readiness.

Matches users with service providers that align with their risk profile, lifestyle needs, and financial priorities.

6.  Business Model

Revenue Streams:

B2C Subscriptions: Homeowners pay for access to advanced AI insights and premium financial recommendations.
B2B SaaS: Landlords, mortgage brokers, and insurance providers license our AI-driven insights to better target their customers.
Vendor Marketplace Fees: Service providers pay for high-quality, behaviorally qualified leads.
Data Monetization: Aggregated, anonymized psychographic trends are licensed to financial institutions.
Unit Economics:

CAC: Targeting efficient acquisition channels, with multi-channel strategies (paid, organic, referral, partnerships) aiming for a CAC in the £7–£20 range as channels mature.
LTV: With an estimated average monthly revenue per user and strong retention strategies, we project an LTV of around £400 per customer—supporting an LTV:CAC ratio of at least 3:1.
