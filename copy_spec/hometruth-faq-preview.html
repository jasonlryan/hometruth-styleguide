Getting Started

How is this different from just Googling my questions?

Google gives you generic advice for generic properties. HomeTruth gives you answers specific to your property, based on your actual records, your maintenance history, and your situation. When you ask "should I worry about my boiler?", we know which boiler you have and when it was last serviced.

What do I need to get started?

Just your address. We'll pull in the basics about your property to get you going. From there, you can upload documents, receipts, photos—anything that matters. The more you add, the better your answers get.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. One property, no time limit, no credit card. You get document storage, basic questions answered, and maintenance reminders. Upgrade to Pro when you want unlimited questions and proactive guidance.

Your Data

Where does my data go?

Your data is stored securely and encrypted. We don't sell it, share it, or use it for anything except giving you better answers about your property. You can export or delete it at any time.

Can my records be changed or deleted by someone else?

No. Once a record is created, it can't be altered or removed. This protects you—especially useful when selling, dealing with insurance, or resolving disputes. Your property history is permanent and tamper-proof.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data stays yours. You can export everything at any time. If you cancel Pro, you keep read access to your records on the free plan. We never hold your data hostage.

How It Works

What kind of questions can I ask?

Anything about your property. "When was my roof last inspected?" "What maintenance should I prioritise this year?" "Am I compliant with current landlord regulations?" "What should I budget for next quarter?" The more records you've uploaded, the more specific and useful the answers.

Does it get smarter over time?

Yes. The more you use HomeTruth, the more it understands your property. Upload a receipt today, and six months from now we'll remind you when the warranty is about to expire. Your records build a complete picture that improves every answer.
