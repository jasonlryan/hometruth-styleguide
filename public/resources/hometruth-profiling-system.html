2–3 minute adaptive onboarding
Purpose

We onboard to build a 3D Human Blueprint.

The profiling system personalizes the entire property journey by understanding what you believe, why you act, and how you feel. It powers an AI-powered personal property assistant that anticipates needs, matches services, and keeps guidance aligned with your comfort zone.

Attitudinal

Risk tolerance, tech affinity, sustainability, and trust posture.

Motivational

Security vs. status, legacy vs. liquidity, urgency, and intent signals.

Emotional

Anxiety triggers, excitement catalysts, and decision-fatigue patterns.

Outcomes we unlock

Proactive Guidance

We nudge before questions arise—deadlines, budgets, documents, and next-best actions.

Hyper-Personalized Matching

Properties, services, contractors, and finance offers filtered by your comfort zone.

Higher Stickiness

An evolving profile makes the assistant feel tailored and familiar every time you return.

Data Monetization (Ethical)

Anonymized insights help partners tailor value—not spam—around the moments you care about.

Why this matters

Less noise, more clarity

We remove 60–80% of irrelevant results and surface only what fits your risk, style, and timeline.

Confidence over speed

Guidance respects anxiety triggers and pacing, replacing pressure with step-by-step checklists.

Trustable recommendations
